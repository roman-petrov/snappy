# ✍️ Editing for skimmers

## 🎯 Tight markers

Lead with **intent**, then _tone_, then **outcome** in short gaps.

Keep `identifiers` beside **actions** and _hints_ in one line: **save** _draft_ `note.md` **publish**.

Mark retired wording with ~~strike~~ next to keepers: ~~WIP~~ **ship** _soon_ `v1`.

Cut mid-thought for the stream: open **bold repair and also _italic repair.

## 🧩 Nested feel

Outer **bold with _inner italic_ still bold** then plain prose.

Strike wrapping code: ~~drop `legacyId` next~~ then continue.

Pack nearby marks: **x** `y` **z** _w_ ~~v~~ without turning the sentence into noise.

## ✅ Closing

End dense on purpose: **one** _two_ **three** `four` ~~five~~ **six**.
